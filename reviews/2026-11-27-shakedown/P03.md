## Omission found

The same Legistar search ("Cato", "Ashland City") shows movement the other way near Cato Road: BL2021-1046 (matter 13882), RS15 to RM9-NS, 3.29 acres on Ashland City Hwy west of Cato Road, Planning Commission approved with conditions 2021-11-18, passed 2022-01-18; BL2023-107 (matter 16709), RS15 to SP, 12.76 acres at 4315 to 4321 Cato Road, five units, passed 2023-12-19; BL2024-251 (matter 17093), Council accepted new sewer mains for Eaton Creek Commons Phase 2 on Ashland City Hwy, passed 2024-03-19. These start from RS15, not AR2a, and their policy areas were not checked. They do not overturn the status, but "applied when land is rezoned" should not read as applied every time.

## Proposed text for operator ruling

> NashvilleNext (2015) set a policy to limit sewer extensions into rural areas. Planning staff cite it when reviewing rural upzonings: two Cato Road upzonings were disapproved by the Planning Commission and withdrawn (2022, 2023). Metro Water's published development guides decide extensions on capacity and developer-built lines and do not mention rural policy. From 2024 to 2026 Council accepted four developer-built sewer mains along Whites Creek Pike and Clarksville Pike; whether those sites are in rural policy was not checked.

The Best Brands sentence is dropped, as WP-003 already orders.
