## Omission found

The row compares the 2022 rule with 50% but leaves out the 15% it replaced and the 33% cluster lot ask (action 174). Both are on primary pages above and make the "weaker form" reading concrete.

## Status test

Test applied (from the Method section of RECOMMENDATIONS-IMPLEMENTATION.md, the only written test found; WP-003 asks for it in the methodology doc, not yet there): Implemented = a binding rule, program or funding that does what was recommended, in substance. Partly implemented = part of it, or a weaker form. No implementation reported = no record found either way, or no measure.

A binding open-space rule for subdivisions exists (2022), at 20% rather than 50%, mandatory under conditions rather than by right, and not in the AG, AR2a, R80 and R40 districts the 2006 bill covered. That is a weaker form.

## Proposed text for operator ruling

> NashvilleNext (2015) asked for a conservation subdivision ordinance by right with at least 50% open space; the 2011 Open Space Plan asked for one by right by 2012. A 2006 bill with 50% open space was withdrawn in 2007. The 2022 Conservation Development rule (BL2022-1121) replaced the 15% cluster lot option: in R and RS zones, on sites large enough and with enough natural features, natural areas up to 20% of the site must be kept, or at least 20% where there are more. It is not written for AR2a or AG zones.

Ruling needed: whether to keep "not written for AR2a or AG zones" pending a check of how 17.28.030.A.2 (20% slopes) is applied, or drop the zone clause.
