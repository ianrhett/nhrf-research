## Proposed text for operator ruling

> Countywide canopy grew 5,247 acres from 2010 to 2021, then dipped by a net 900 acres from 2016 to 2021: the urban services district lost about 1,840 acres while the rest of the county gained about 940. Rural (T2) canopy grew and is above its 65% goal. A permanent tree fund pays for planting, including on private land, but no rule protects large forested tracts on private land unless they are being subdivided.
