## Omission found

BL2022-1121, Section 5 (passed 2022-05-17) rewrote 17.28.030.A.2: single and two-family subdivisions on 20 percent or greater slopes must use the conservation development provisions of 17.12.090, with lots grouped on slopes under 20 percent and large areas over 25 percent recorded as open space. The ordinance also lists contiguous 20 percent slopes of 10,000 sq ft or more as land to preserve under conservation development. This is a delivered hillside step after NashvilleNext. The row cites BL2022-1121 as evidence, but the text never mentions it. It supports "Partly implemented" and belongs in what_was_done.

## Proposed text for operator ruling

> Rural subdivision rules protect contiguous slopes over 15% when land under rural policy is subdivided. In 2022 Metro required subdivisions on slopes of 20% or more to use conservation design (BL2022-1121). Countywide, single and two-family grading standards still begin at 20%, single and two-family lots of an acre or more are exempt from the lot standards, and the 15% hillside standard NashvilleNext asked Metro to begin work on within 1 to 3 years (2015) has not been adopted for homes.

Ruling needed before use: the 2026-11-01 ruling bars public claims about how strong or weak current Metro law protects the forest. Clauses 2 and 3 describe current law.
