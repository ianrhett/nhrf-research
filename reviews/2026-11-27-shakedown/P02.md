## Status test

Test applied (Method section of RECOMMENDATIONS-IMPLEMENTATION.md; WP-003 asks for it in the methodology doc, not yet there): Implemented = a binding rule, program or funding that does what was recommended, in substance. Partly implemented = part of it, or a weaker form. No implementation reported = no record found either way, or no measure.

What exists for the Rim as such is a 2025 naming resolution, an unfunded master plan line, and a strategy under way. The binding piece the row relies on, the 2016 rural subdivision rules, applies by policy area, not to the Rim, and its coverage of the Rim is not measured (claim 1). Whether that counts as "part of" preserving the Rim depends on the coverage figure the row cannot yet support.

Old status: Partly implemented. Proposed status: Partly implemented, held pending a coverage figure. If the operator rules that coverage must be measured before it is claimed, the remaining record (naming, unfunded plan) is closer to No implementation reported. Operator ruling needed.

## Proposed text for operator ruling

> No adopted Metro map or rule names the Highland Rim. Rural and Conservation policy, which guides rezoning and triggers the 2016 rural subdivision rules, covers parts of it (share not measured), and those policies "are not regulatory." In October 2025 Council recognized the area as "Nashville's Highland Rim Forest" (recognition only; no boundary, rules or funding). A $500,000 Highland Rim Forests master plan has been in the capital budget since 2024, status "Not started," and was not funded in the FY2027 Capital Spending Plan. The Ecological Conservation Strategy, due fall 2027, uses the Rim as a pilot area.
