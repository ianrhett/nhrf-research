| 2 | ...with scenic easements required when land along them is subdivided | VERIFIED, needs scope | Subdivision Regulations (amended 2021-01-21), 3-10.5 "Relationship to Scenic Routes", PDF p47 to p48: a subdivision "fronting or encompassing a street designated on the Major Street Plan as a scenic arterial" must designate the lot area "within 75 feet of the centerline of the existing right-of-way ... on the face of the plat as a 'Scenic Easement'", with a plat note barring disturbance of "natural features ... within this easement except driveway crossings." Applies at subdivision only. The regulations were amended again June 27, 2024 (per the implementation notes); the 2024 text was not opened. |

Not searched: Planning Department studies; the 1980s to 2010s record outside Legistar. |
| 4 | ...or protected | PARTLY SUPPORTED (absence claim) | Same search.
