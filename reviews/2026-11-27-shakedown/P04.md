## Omission found

Upzonings in rural areas also passed, for example BL2023-1930 (matter 16107), AR2A to RS30, 1.8 acres at Whites Creek Pike and Laws Road, passed 2023-07-18. The row lists only movement in one direction. One small case does not change the status, but a fair summary says rezonings went both ways.

## Status test

Test applied (Method section of RECOMMENDATIONS-IMPLEMENTATION.md; WP-003 asks for it in the methodology doc, not yet there): Implemented = a binding rule, program or funding that does what was recommended, in substance. Partly implemented = part of it, or a weaker form. No implementation reported = no record found either way, or no measure.

A binding subdivision rule for T2 Rural policy areas exists (2016), and a few Planning-initiated downzonings passed. The specific tool asked for in 2011 and 2015, an agricultural zoning category or overlay, was not found within the searched scope. That is part of what was asked.

Old status: Partly implemented. Proposed status: Partly implemented (no change).

## Proposed text for operator ruling

> Since 2016, subdivisions in rural policy areas must follow the Rural Character Subdivision rules. Planning-initiated rezonings lowered density on about 690 acres in 2021 to 2023 (BL2021-944, BL2021-954, BL2022-1646), and two Cato Road upzonings were disapproved by the Planning Commission and withdrawn (2022, 2023). No agricultural zoning category, asked for in 2015, or farm overlay, asked for in 2011, was found in Council legislation since 2020.

Note: "about 690 acres" is 652.5 + 32.15 + 3 = 687.65 acres from the three bill titles above.
