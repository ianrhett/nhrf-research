## Proposed text for operator ruling

> Metro requires no-disturb buffers along streams, which its stormwater reports describe as protecting all aquatic habitat. Since 2022, subdivisions on designated cedar glade land must make "all reasonable effort" to preserve it through conservation development (Metro Code 17.28.060, BL2022-1121). No Metro rule was found for the Mill Creek habitat overlay, in-county wetland mitigation, or invasive species asked for.
