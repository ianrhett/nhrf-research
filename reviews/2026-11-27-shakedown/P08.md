## Omission found

1. The same NashvilleNext 2016 Annual Report page (PDF p32) says The Land Trust for Tennessee "In recent years protected 445 acres in the Beaman to Bells Bend Corridor with conservation easements." That is a nonprofit figure, larger than Metro's 290 acres, and should not be merged with it.
2. NashvilleNext action 224 (NR 1.2 and BE 2.1, Near term; Vol. 4 PDF p70 and p51) asked to fund the Open Space Acquisition Fund "with at least $5 million". P10 covers the fund.

## Internal-consistency carry-over (pass 1)

## Proposed text for operator ruling

> Metro buys Rim land when opportunities arise: Beaman Park grew by 568 acres in 2015, five conservation easements protected 290 acres in 2009, and in November 2026 Council approved an option to buy 300 acres next to Beaman Park from The Conservation Fund (RS2026-2410); closing not yet confirmed. There is no standing acquisition program. Metro reports $12.8 million allocated for park land since 2017; Plan to Play 2017 put the market value of its recommended park land at $534 million; the two are different measures.
