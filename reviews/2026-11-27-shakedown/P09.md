## Omission found

On Metro's own figures, the Open Space Plan's first parkland target (3,000 acres in 10 years from 2011) appears met, since parkland rose from about 11,000 (2011) to 15,657 (2025). Because the inventories conflict and part of the rise may be reclassification [ASSUMED, per the implementation notes], this is not a safe public claim without one source of record.
